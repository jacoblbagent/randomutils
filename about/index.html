About RandomUtils

Run by jacoblbagent, with no company behind it.

RandomUtils is a small set of single-purpose tools: convert a DVR recording or a QuickTime clip to MP4, extract a video’s audio, generate a UUID, or work out compound interest. Each does one job.

Everything runs in your browser

The video converters run on FFmpeg compiled to WebAssembly, the UUID generator uses the browser’s cryptographic random source, and the calculator is arithmetic in the page. Files are read and written on your own machine, with no upload step and no server to upload to. There are no accounts, no queue on someone else’s disk, and no copy of your file anywhere you cannot see. Close the tab and it is gone.

That matters for the files people bring here: a DVR recording or a work clip you would rather not hand to a stranger. A conversion site means trusting it to delete the file afterwards. Here nothing leaves your device, and the tools keep working offline, on a plane or on a locked-down laptop, once the page has loaded.

What the site does not do

  • Upload, store, log or forward your files. There is no upload endpoint.
  • Ask for an account, or even a name.
  • Run analytics on your files, or on you. The only third party is the ad service.
  • Install anything, request unusual permissions, or keep a copy after the tab closes.
  • Watermark the output or cap how many files you convert.

The few preferences the browser keeps are listed on the privacy page.

How it is paid for

Free, with no paid tier. Ads served by Google AdSense pay for it, and that is the only third party the page loads. No sponsored recommendations, no affiliate links, no upsell. The privacy page covers the ad cookies.

Source and contact

The source is public: read it, file an issue, or run your own copy: github.com/jacoblbagent. For a broken conversion, a format request, an ad question or a takedown, email jacoblbagent@gmail.com, or see the contact page for what to include. Replies are best effort, usually within a few days.
