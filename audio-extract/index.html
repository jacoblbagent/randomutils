About this tool

WAV or MP3?

  • WAV: uncompressed 16-bit PCM at the source’s own sample rate. Lossless and accepted by every editor, at roughly 10 MB per minute stereo. Choose it when you will edit, transcribe or re-encode the audio later.
  • MP3: 192 kbps, transparent enough for speech and fine for most music, about a tenth the WAV’s size and plays on anything. Choose it when the audio is the finished product: a podcast, a voice memo, a soundtrack for a phone.

Where the audio comes from

  • Audio comes from the first audio track and is re-encoded to the format you pick. For a normal video with one track, that is the audio.
  • A video with several tracks, such as multiple language dubs, yields the first. For another track, note it on the contact page.
  • A video with no audio track fails rather than producing a silent file.
  • Audio files work as input too: WAV→MP3 or MP3→WAV is a format conversion.

Formats it accepts

Video: MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM, AVI, FLV, WMV, MPEG, TS, M2TS, 3GP, OGV, VOB, MXF and more. Audio: MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, FLAC, OGG, Opus, WMA, AIFF and more. Anything else is skipped and named above the queue.

If something goes wrong

  • The job fails at “Extracting audio”. Usually no audio track, or a codec the bundled FFmpeg cannot decode. Try another file to confirm the tool itself is fine.
  • The WAV is enormous. It is uncompressed. Extract to MP3 if size matters more than fidelity.
  • A rental or store-bought download fails. DRM files are unreadable by ordinary tools, and are not yours to convert.
  • A very long video stops part-way. The file and its output live in page memory, so a video over roughly 2 GB may run out of room.

Still stuck? The contact page lists exactly what to include.
