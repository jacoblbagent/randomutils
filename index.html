Browser tools that never upload your files

Small single-purpose tools that run entirely in your browser. No accounts, no uploads, no server holding your files.

TS → MP4

Convert .ts recordings to MP4
Drop DVR and over-the-air captures in and get MP4s that play anywhere, stream-copied where possible. Runs on FFmpeg in your browser; nothing is uploaded.Video · FFmpeg · Offline

MOV → MP4

Convert QuickTime .mov files to MP4
Drop QuickTime movies in and get MP4s that play anywhere: H.264, HEVC and AAC copied untouched, ProRes, DV and PCM re-encoded. Nothing is uploaded.Video · FFmpeg · Offline

Video → Audio

Extract a video’s audio as WAV or MP3
Drop a video in, pick WAV or MP3, and download the audio track. No upload, no editing suite.Audio · Video · FFmpeg · Offline

UUID generator

Version 4 and version 7 UUIDs, made in this tab
Pick v4 for pure randomness, or v7 for ids that start with a millisecond timestamp and sort by creation time. Up to 100 at once, with hyphens, braces and case as options.Developer · UUID · Offline

Compound interest

What a balance grows to with monthly contributions
A starting amount plus a monthly deposit at a fixed annual rate, with a year-by-year table and a toggle for the currency symbol. Nothing is uploaded.Finance · Calculator · Offline
The video tools run on FFmpeg compiled to WebAssembly, the UUID generator uses your browser’s own crypto, and the interest calculator is plain arithmetic in the page. Nothing you open or enter is uploaded, and nothing survives closing the tab. How that works.
