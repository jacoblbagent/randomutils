Privacy policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

RandomUtils is a set of tools that run inside your browser. Every file you open is processed on your own machine by FFmpeg compiled to WebAssembly, every UUID is drawn from your browser’s own random source, and the interest calculator is arithmetic in the page. Nothing is uploaded, transmitted or stored: no account, no server-side processing, no copy of your file leaving your device.

What stays on your device

  • Files you drop into a converter are read locally. Results are produced in the page and handed back as downloads.
  • UUIDs are generated in the page by your browser’s cryptographic random source and copied only when you press Copy. No list is kept and nothing is written to storage.
  • The queue and the results live in the page’s memory. Closing or reloading the tab discards them.
  • A few preferences are saved in localStorage so a reload keeps them: the feature-flag switches (randomutils.flags), and each converter’s starred files and removed list. They stay in your browser, are not sent anywhere, and clearing site data removes them.
  • We cannot see your files: there is no upload endpoint, no analytics on them, and no copy on any server we control.

Advertising

The site is funded by ads served by Google AdSense, the only third party the page loads. AdSense uses cookies:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites.
  • Google’s advertising cookies let it and its partners show you ads based on your visit here and elsewhere.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ad Settings . Google describes what it collects in its advertising and partner-sites policy .
  • Other vendors and ad networks may place or read cookies, or use web beacons, when an ad is served. Where the law requires consent, Google’s consent tooling collects it before personalised ads are shown; declining still shows ads, just not personalised ones.

The site sets no cookie of its own, runs no analytics and builds no profile. The publisher id is public in ads.txt.

Hosting and server logs

The site is a static build hosted on GitHub Pages and served at randomutils.com. Serving a page normally means the host records the request (IP address, user agent, timestamp) for security and delivery. That logging is GitHub’s, not ours; see GitHub’s privacy statement. We do not collect or process that data, and nothing in a tool session reaches the host.

Children

The site has no accounts, uploads or sign-up, and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If what the site loads or stores changes, this page and the date at the top change with it.
